Real complaints from 2024, and what actually happened

The model learned from 2022–23 and never saw these. Each one shows its call next to what the company really did — including the ones it got wrong.

Black bars are details the CFPB hides before publishing (names, account numbers, dates). Inside a single company, the model ranks about 82 of every 100 paid/unpaid pairs correctly. Many misses are company decisions — a goodwill refund, a firm refusal — that the complaint text simply doesn't show.
